Next steps

Train staff before the launch email goes out, so whoever picks up the phone can answer "how do I sign in?" on day one.

1

App and folder structure discussion with Yolanda, Marissa and Katie

A working session to walk through the app and how client folders are set up in Egnyte. About 30 minutes.

  • Walk through the app from the client side and the staff side.
  • Review the Egnyte folder structure: the client uploads folder, the new firm-to-client folder, and the dated day folders.
  • Confirm what changes in Yolanda's folder template so new matters get the right folders.
  • Capture any feedback in the change log on this page.
2

Pre-launch email

A heads-up only. Clients don't need to do anything yet.

  • Send 2 to 3 weeks before launch, once the stakeholders approve the wording.
  • Send a test to yourself from Settings and read it on your phone first.
  • Send the email to every client with an open matter. Texts go only to clients who opted in.
3

Staff training

Before the launch email, so staff are a step ahead of clients.

  • One 45-minute session, recorded for anyone who misses it.
  • Everyone signs in on their phone and desktop during the session, not afterwards.
  • Cover replying to messages, saved replies, assigning tasks with documents, upload alerts, sending documents to clients, and the incident journal.
  • Practise on the TESTING matter: each person sends a message, assigns a task and uploads a document.
  • Point staff to Help & Guides in Settings for anything after the session.
4

Launch email

The one that gets clients signed in.

  • Contains the app download links, their user ID, the Create your password button, and written step-by-step instructions.
  • Send in batches of about 25 a day rather than all at once, so staff can keep up with questions and it stays well inside the daily Egnyte limit as clients open their folders for the first time.
  • Two business days later, check App Usage, then "Who hasn't joined", and resend those invites by text.
5

Existing-client training

Most clients won't come to a session, so make help available in the forms they actually use.

  • A 2- to 3-minute screen-recorded video, linked from the launch email.
  • A one-page printable guide (PDF) for the front desk and in-person meetings.
  • Two optional 15-minute group video calls in launch week.
  • The app's own first-sign-in walkthrough covers the rest.

Pre-launch announcement

The "Send pre-launch announcement" tool in Settings sends this to clients with open matters, by email and optionally by text. It has a launch-date field (blank until set), a preview, and a "Send a test to me" button. Clients get one of two emails, depending on whether they've signed in to the client portal before. The text message is the same for everyone. Highlighted fields fill in automatically.

What we're changing from Thomas and Sharon's review

Each concern raised in the review, and how the app now handles it.

Concern or suggestionWhat we're doingNotes
App error when assigning a taskFixed first, with an automated test that repeats the exact steps.
A place for documents the firm sends to clientsA new folder in each client's Egnyte folder, set in Settings, with dated "- Sent" day folders. The client gets one push and email per batch: "Your legal team sent documents for review". Off until you turn it on.
Attaching documents when assigning a taskStaff can attach files to a task. They go in the same folder, and the client's task becomes "review this document" with Looks correct and Report an issue.
Files saved straight into EgnytePicked up within about 15 minutes, moved into that day's folder, and the client is notified. Nothing is ever deleted.
Staff not knowing a client uploaded somethingAn immediate push and email to the matter's attorney, paralegal and notify list, replacing the 8 am digest. Uploads close together are grouped into one alert.
Clients struggling to sign inThe invite states their user ID and has a "Create your password" button. The app gets "First time here?" and "Forgot password?". Written setup steps go in every invite email and text.
Staff seeing $0 trust when a client owes moneyStaff alerts and the matter screen show the outstanding balance as well as trust.
Staff still working in the portalA setting hides portal messaging and checklists for staff only, pointing them to the app. Nothing is deleted, and clients aren't affected.
Telling clients the app is comingThe pre-launch announcement above, sent from Settings by email and text, with a preview and a test send first.
Desktop app for staffAn internal Windows installer is built now. The Mac app ships with the iPhone release.
Human error matching clients to Egnyte foldersAlready guarded: one matter per folder, an immediate email when two clients share a name, a waiting list for former clients' folders, and a "Check every open matter" report. Each staff matter screen will also show the linked folder so a wrong link is easy to spot. This replaces moving OneDrive uploads by hand.
Client terms and FAQUpdated to cover documents from the firm, review tasks and password setup.
